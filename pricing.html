Getting started

No registration required

🎓
During the free period, LADOCK is available to everyone for non-commercial use — no institutional email, registration, or license key is needed. Just download and run; the app activates the free academic license automatically until December 31, 2030.

The only limit is commercial use: companies, CROs, pharmaceutical and biotech firms, or any for-profit R&D require a paid commercial license — contact laode_aman@ung.ac.id.
License Timeline

What happens and when

2024 — Now
LADOCK launches · Academic free period begins
Everyone gets full access to LADOCK Desktop at no cost for non-commercial use — no registration needed. Commercial licensing available upon request.
2024 – 2030
Academic Free Period
Anyone can use LADOCK free of charge for non-commercial research, study, and teaching. Only commercial (for-profit) users require a paid license.
January 1, 2031
Academic discount pricing begins
The free academic period ends. Existing and new academic users transition to a discounted institutional license. Contact us for renewal terms and pricing.
